About us

At RTA, our customers and community are the key of everything we do.

RecycleTech Australia (RTA) is a Queensland-based tyre recycling company specialising in truck and OTR tyre recycling, with a new site opening in Mackay. Our mission is to contribute to environmental sustainability by recycling tyres into valuable materials, thereby reducing landfill waste and promoting the reuse of resources. Our business model involves collecting used tyres from various sources, processing them through efficient recycling methods, and supplying the resulting products to manufacturers and consumers.

Our primary customers include:

Australian flexible pavement industry using recycled rubber for road paving Australian manufacturers using recycled crumb rubber for building materials, such as tile grouts and adhesives Australian companies using recycled rubber for flooring and sportsfields Manufacturer using recycled steel in production processes for railway parts Retail consumers purchasing recycled rubber products for landscaping
