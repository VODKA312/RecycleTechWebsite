Follow Us

RecycleTech Australia (RTA) is a Queensland-based tyre recycling company focusing on truck and OTR tyre recycling. With a mission to enhance environmental sustainability, RTA collects used tyres and processes them into valuable materials. Key customers include the flexible pavement industry, manufacturers of building materials, and retailers of recycled rubber products. RTA has a strong reputation for supplying high-quality 30-mesh crumb rubber, used in various Queensland road surfacing projects. By recycling end-of-life tyres, RTA contributes significantly to Queensland's circular economy.
