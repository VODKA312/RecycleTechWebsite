01.Company Overview

Recycletech Australia Pty Ltd is a vertically integrated tyre recycling company headquartered in Queensland, with two operational facilities located in Mackay and Carole Park (Brisbane). We specialise in converting end-of-life tyres — including mining-grade OTR tyres — into high-quality recycled materials used in infrastructure, manufacturing, and environmental applications.

With a total processing capacity of 8,000 tonnes annually, Recycletech operates a closed-loop system covering collection, shredding, separation, material conversion, and delivery. Recycletech reduces landfill waste by over 1,000 tonnes annually, through the processing of mining-grade tyres that would otherwise be discarded — a contribution we take pride in as part of our commitment to real-world sustainability.

By recovering and reprocessing these large-format tyres, we help close the loop on one of Australia’s most complex waste streams, transforming environmental risk into infrastructure-grade resources.

02.National Accreditation

National Accreditation – TSA 4.5 Star Rating

Tyre Stewardship Australia (TSA) is the national stewardship body endorsed by the Australian Government to oversee the environmental management of end-of-life tyres. Its framework sets industry-wide expectations for compliance, traceability, and circular resource outcomes.

TSA’s performance rating system is independently audited and used as a benchmark across government procurement, ESG screening, commercial credit reviews, and infrastructure supply chains. Scores reflect verified performance in regulatory compliance, downstream responsibility, material traceability, and resource recovery efficiency.

Recycletech is the only tyre recycler in Australia currently rated 4.5 stars under TSA’s national framework — the highest score awarded to date.

We actively align our operations with TSA standards and incorporate its guidelines into our internal compliance and reporting systems.

This rating reflects our operational integrity, but also reinforces our role in setting consistent, measurable standards within the tyre recovery sector.

03.Our Fleet

Our logistics are fully in-house, supported by a fleet of 10 specialised trucks, comprising 10, 12, and 14-pallet heavy rigid vehicles and purpose-configured B-double combinations capable of transporting oversized mining-grade OTR tyres. This enables responsive, high-volume fulfilment and end-to-end control across both regional and metropolitan areas.

04.Community & Council Impact

Recycletech’s recycled materials are indirectly used in local government infrastructure projects across Queensland, via supply to national contractors, civil engineering firms, and waste service providers. Our products are incorporated into road bases, footpaths, and public works through trusted downstream applications.

Through this model, we contribute to community infrastructure, reduce landfill dependency, and support government-aligned circular economy objectives in practice — not just in principle.
