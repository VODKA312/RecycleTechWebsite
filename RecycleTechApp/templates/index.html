Recycletech Australia Pty Ltd is a Queensland-based tyre recycling and circular manufacturing company. The business holds a 4.5-star environmental performance rating from Tyre Stewardship Australia (TSA), the highest rating in Queensland and among the top scores nationally.

We operate two specialised facilities with a combined annual processing capacity of 8,000 tonnes of tyres and by-products:

The Mackay facility is dedicated to ultra-large mining tyre recycling, addressing one of the most technically demanding waste streams in Australia.

The Brisbane facility focuses on high-volume processing and material distribution, supporting infrastructure development and industrial supply chains.

Our operations prevent over 1,500 tonnes of mining tyres from entering landfill annually. A dedicated fleet of eight specialised trucks ensures efficient tyre collection and delivery across Queensland and nearby regions.

Recycletech also maintains close working relationships with multiple local councils, providing reliable recycling solutions that align with government sustainability goals.

Recycletech is focused on scalable recycling technology, measurable environmental outcomes, and strong partnerships, positioning the company as a key contributor to Australia’s resource recovery sector.
